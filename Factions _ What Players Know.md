# Factions

These are the organisations operating openly in Lunaria. Every player character would know of them; most would have encountered at least one.

---

## The Seekers' Concord & Runners
The world's primary below-exploration and long-distance courier network. Two distinct arms: the Seekers' Concord funds and organises Below expeditions, academic research, and geographic survey work. The Runners are the operational field branch - the people who actually go below the Threshold Line, carry messages between kingdoms that don't trust the oracle relay, and find things that other people can't find.

Runners are the most respected independent professionals in Lunaria. They are also among the most dangerous to cross. Runner neutrality is institutional - they serve whoever hires them without political preference - but the Concord's leadership has strong views about the Below that occasionally surface in which expeditions get funded.

**Public presence:** Runner wayhouses in every major city. Concord offices in Verath-Ul and Nassem's Seat. Well-known contract rates posted publicly.

---

## The Pale Quill (Verath Intelligence)
The Conclave of Verath's intelligence service - and the operators of the oracle relay, the only near-instant long-distance communication in the world. The Pale Quill is publicly acknowledged as existing; its specific operations are not.

The oracle relay allows messages between Pale Quill nodes in six major cities to travel in minutes rather than weeks. Public oracle consultations are available at a significant premium with a three-month waiting list. The Pale Quill's field operatives are technically neutral diplomatic staff - technically.

**Public presence:** Oracle consultation offices in every capital. The Pale Quarter in Verath-Ul. Widely acknowledged as present everywhere.

---

## The Ascendant Church
The Dominion of Aethon's state faith, with significant presence in Vethara Cluster settlements and Aethon-aligned territories. Theology: the Calamity was a divine elevation - the worthy were raised into the sky. The Church controls succession endorsement in Aethon, operates the world's largest parish registration network, and holds approximately forty percent of civilian deposits through its banking orders.

Outside the Dominion, the Church has presence but not authority. In Verath-Ul academic circles, its theology is considered historically interesting. In Freehold, it is not popular.

**Public presence:** Grand Cathedral in Aethon Prime. Parish churches throughout the Dominion. Chapels in most major trading cities.

---

## The Penitent Way
A faith tradition found across all kingdoms, strongest in the Ashenmere and among communities that experienced the Calamity's aftermath directly. Doctrine: the dead exist in the specificity of how they are remembered. General impression keeps no one. A specific memory - the exact quality of a laugh, a particular gesture - maintains the person in the living world.

Penitent Way chapter houses maintain the Book of Names: records of the dead not captured in official registers. The Penitent Way has no political ambitions and significant moral authority.

**Public presence:** Chapter houses in most major cities. Recognisable by their plain exteriors and the Penitent Way symbol - a hand, open, palm outward.

---

## The Groundwalker Faith
A minority faith with strongest presence in the Ashkin Republic and Fringe communities. Holds that the Below is not evil but not safe - that presence near it requires preparation and integrity. Practitioners develop specific Below-adjacent disciplines and maintain the most organised harm-reduction approach to Below-proximity of any institution in the sky.

The Groundwalker faith's botanical research arm has unusual plant acquisition requests active with several kingdoms' botanical collections.

**Public presence:** Chapter houses primarily in Fringe communities and the Republic. Recognisable by earth-tone robes.

---

## Vareth & Co.
The sky's largest independent commercial firm. Trade facilitation, commercial intelligence, sovereign debt. Offices in every major city. Their Aethon Prime headquarters is deliberately more impressive than any government building of equivalent function. This is not an accident.

Vareth & Co. is widely known to have better commercial intelligence than most governments. How they obtain it is a matter of professional speculation. Their commercial paper and market analyses are the most trusted in the sky.

**Public presence:** Major offices in every capital. Highly visible, highly professional.

---

## The Counting House & Tidewrack
A commercial network and its associated smuggling operation, operating across all kingdoms. The Counting House provides financial services - freight assessment, commercial insurance, letters of credit - that are legitimate and useful. The Tidewrack is the smuggling arm that moves embargo goods, controlled materials, and anything else that needs to cross borders without documentation.

The relationship between the Counting House and the Tidewrack is an open secret in commercial circles. Neither acknowledges the other officially.

**Public presence:** Counting House offices in major commercial districts, no signage but known to those who need them. Tidewrack contacts available through Working Docks networks in port cities.

---

## The Founding Families
Fourteen prominent families with historical presence in every major kingdom's commercial and social structure. Not an organisation in any formal sense - a network of relationships and shared commercial interest that has been operating for four centuries. The Founding Families are known in commercial and noble circles; their coordinated nature is known but not publicly documented.

If you're doing significant commercial activity in any major city, you've probably dealt with a Founding Families associate without knowing it.

**Public presence:** Embedded in commercial and noble social structures in every kingdom. No office, no sign, no membership card.
