# Character Creation Guide
*Everything you need to build a character for Lunaria*

---

## Before You Start — What Kind of World Is This?

Lunaria is a grounded investigation campaign with escalating stakes. The world has:

- **Real politics.** Factions have genuine agendas that affect your character's life whether they want them to or not.
- **A specific threat.** The Below is rising. This is not background — it is the campaign's engine.
- **Earned power.** You begin as capable people in a complicated world, not chosen heroes. You become significant through what you do, not what you are.
- **Moral weight.** There are no clean solutions. The people in the right don't always win. Decisions have lasting consequences.

**The tone is:** tense investigation, coalition politics, Below-horror, and moments of genuine warmth between people who chose to trust each other.

---

## Step 1 — Choose Your Class

All standard 5e classes work in Lunaria. The following classes from **Valda's Spire of Secrets** are also available:

| VSS Class | Lunaria Fit |
|---|---|
| **Investigator** | Built for this campaign. Clue mechanics, broad spell list, evidence-synthesis. |
| **Necromancer** | Below-adjacent content makes this deeply relevant. Charnel Touch, Seance. |
| **Witch** | The three Covens make witch characters politically rich. Choose a Coven. |
| **Warden** | Threshold Line defenders. Sentinel identity. Below-country survival. |
| **Captain** | Coalition-building campaigns reward the Captain's leadership mechanics. |
| **Alchemist** | Fragment-chemistry and field science. Works well for Concord-affiliated characters. |

**Starting Level:** 1, milestoning to 5 across Arc 1.

## Step 2 — Choose Your Background

Standard backgrounds work. The following Lunaria-specific backgrounds are available:

### FRINGE COMMUNITY MEMBER
*You grew up near the Threshold Line.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Survival, Medicine
**Tool Proficiencies:** Healer's kit, one artisan tool
**Equipment:** Fringe-issue rope (50ft), bone-ash filter cloth, healer's kit, community watch token, 12gp

**Feature — Below Proximity:** Fringe communities notice you as one of their own. In any Fringe or Below-adjacent community, you find food, basic shelter, and honest information. You also know the Threshold Line measurement rope convention — you can read knot-counts and understand what they mean without being told.

**Suggested character questions:** Why did you leave (or why are you still there)? Who did you lose to Fragment Fever or Below-creature activity? What does the Watch Tower mean to you?

---

### REPUBLIC CITIZEN
*You were born into the Ashkin Republic's civic tradition.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** History, Persuasion
**Tool Proficiencies:** Calligrapher's tools, one gaming set
**Equipment:** Republic founding document (personal copy), Night of Chains commemorative token, community gathering schedule, 15gp

**Feature — Voice and Vote:** In any Republic Assembly context or official civic proceeding, you have the right to speak and be heard. This is not persuasion — it is procedural. You know how to use Republic institutional processes and cannot be procedurally excluded from them.

**Suggested character questions:** What does the Night of Chains mean to you personally? Do you have a name on the Wall? What brought you beyond Freehold's community?

---

### BELOW-RUNNER
*You worked with or trained in the Seekers' Concord.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics, Investigation
**Tool Proficiencies:** Navigator's tools, cartographer's tools
**Equipment:** Runner wayhouse token (valid for lodging at any Concord wayhouse), depth rope (100ft, reinforced), descent harness, 20gp

**Feature — Runner Neutrality:** Runner protocols grant you entry to Below-adjacent areas that other professions cannot access. You can move through politically contested territory carrying Runner credentials without being stopped by any party who respects Concord neutrality (most do). You know Concord signal codes.

**Suggested character questions:** Why did you go below? What did you find? Why did you stop (or did you)?

---

### INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLAR
*You trained at or with Verath-Ul's academic institutions.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** History, Arcana
**Tool Proficiencies:** Calligrapher's tools, one language (pre-Calamity notation)
**Equipment:** Academic Post research credential, partial pre-Calamity language dictionary, field notebook (50 pages, annotated), 14gp

**Feature — Archive Access:** With your credential, you can access the Grand Archive's public and semi-restricted collections without appointment. Scholars across Lunaria recognise your credential as legitimate. In research and investigation contexts, you can request materials that non-credentialed individuals cannot access.

**Suggested character questions:** What were you researching? What did you find that you weren't supposed to? Who at the Archive knows you?

---

### KAIRAN MERCHANT FACTOR
*You worked in Kairan commercial networks.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Persuasion, Insight
**Tool Proficiencies:** Navigator's tools, Calligrapher's tools
**Equipment:** Letter of credit (50gp equivalent, redeemable at any Nassem Exchange clearing office), commercial identity seal, Kairan trade-route charts, 25gp

**Feature — The Deal Network:** You have contacts in the Nassem Exchange's commercial registry. Once per arc, you can call in a contact to provide: commercial intelligence on any major trade entity, a letter of introduction to a Kairan-affiliated business, or access to the secondary market for controlled goods (no questions asked, significant premium).

**Suggested character questions:** What went wrong with a deal? Who do you owe and who owes you? Why did you leave the commercial network?

---

### STONEHOLLOW CRAFTSPERSON
*You trained in the Compact's Deepforge tradition.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Investigation, History
**Tool Proficiencies:** Smith's tools or mason's tools, one artisan tool
**Equipment:** Deepforge quality certification token, sample of Grade B lift-stone (inert), masterwork tool of your specialty, 18gp

**Feature — The Compact's Mark:** Work you produce is identifiable as Stonehollow-quality. This opens doors in commercial contexts — buyers pay premium, institutions grant access, and other craftspeople treat you as a peer regardless of your surface-world status. You can also assess any construction or manufactured object's structural integrity as a free action.

**Suggested character questions:** Why did you leave Deephold? What did the Long Memory teach you about yourself? What are you making?

---

### CHURCH ORDAINED
*You hold a position in the Ascendant Church.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Religion, Persuasion
**Tool Proficiencies:** Calligrapher's tools, healer's kit
**Equipment:** Church ordination papers, Church-standard prayer book, silver Church sigil, access to any Church parish for shelter, 12gp

**Feature — Institutional Doors:** Church credentials open institutional access in the Dominion of Aethon and allied territories that would otherwise require significant political capital. Parish records, Church-held historical documents, and administrative access to Church-affiliated buildings are available to you. Note: the Church's theology has specific and known gaps. What you do with that knowledge is your business.

**Suggested character questions:** What do you genuinely believe? What have you been asked to suppress or ignore? What are you trying to understand?

---

### PENITENT WAY VOLUNTEER
*You work in the Way's community service and memory tradition.*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Medicine, Insight
**Tool Proficiencies:** Healer's kit, one language (any)
**Equipment:** Penitent Way chapter token, Book of Names (personal volume, 20 recorded), community contact network across 3 kingdoms, 10gp

**Feature — The Name Network:** Penitent Way chapters exist in every major city. Using your token, you can access lodging, introductions to local practitioners, and community intelligence (who is in distress, who needs help, what has changed recently) in any city with a chapter house. The Way's political neutrality means this access is not compromised by faction affiliations.

**Suggested character questions:** Whose name brought you to the Way? What do you carry from the people you remember? What does "specific" mean to you?

## Step 3 — The Gods (What Your Character Knows)

The Three Courts of Divinity are common knowledge in the sense that everyone knows the gods exist and has heard the major ones' names. Deep theological knowledge is not common — it is specific to clerics, scholars, and devoted practitioners.

**What any character knows:**

The gods are real. They do not walk the world. They communicate through dreams, omens, blessings, and very occasionally through miracles significant enough that kingdoms notice. They organised themselves into three Courts after the First Collision — the theological event that created the world. The Veil Divine separates them from direct contact.

**The most commonly known gods by kingdom:**

| Kingdom | Primary gods in common use |
|---|---|
| Aethon | Solmaren (absorbed into Church as "The Light of Elevation"), Tharren (justice tradition) |
| Verath | Lyreth (archivists), Ossiveth (researchers), Morreth (intelligence tradition) |
| Kairan | Mireth (fair exchange — embedded in commercial practice), Caldren (navigation) |
| Stonehollow | Thyremoth (stone and deep memory), Aelindra (craft tradition) |
| Myren | Aelindra (beauty and making), Kaseth (fate — Salt Court tradition) |
| Republic | Brennan (community and hearth), Tharren (justice and the Wall) |
| Verdant Reach | Vael (life), Calven (wilderness and animals) |

**What characters do NOT commonly know:**
- The gods' specific schemes and current agendas
- The divine political conflicts and which gods are aligned against each other
- That specific institutions (the Grand Archive, the Office of Sealed Inquiry, the Penitent Way) are directly shaped by specific gods
- Any god's relationship to the convergence or to Vasareth's current state

**Cleric characters:** You chose your god and know their domain. You know your god's public theology, their holy symbol, and the standard liturgical tradition. You may have received personal divine communication — but if so, it was probably through Aureathis's dream-space, and it may not have been clear.

## Step 4 — Starting Equipment and Currency

### Currency
The Lunarian commercial system uses:
- **Gold Marks (GM):** Standard unit. Nassem Exchange certified. Accepted everywhere.
- **Silver Chips:** Everyday currency. 10 silver chips = 1 Gold Mark.
- **Copper Bits:** Small purchases. 10 copper bits = 1 silver chip.
- **Kairan Commercial Paper:** Letters of credit redeemable at Exchange offices. Standard for large transactions. Worth face value + 2% Exchange fee to redeem in cash.
- **Crown Marks (Aethon):** Imperial currency, also widely accepted. 1:1 with Gold Marks in most commercial contexts.

**Starting wealth by background:** As per equipment listed above, plus class starting equipment (standard 5e). If taking coin instead, consult your DM for background-appropriate amounts.

## Step 5 — The World You're Entering

You are starting the campaign in or near the Ashkin Republic's Fringe communities, in Thirdmonth of Year 100.

**What your character knows about current events:**
- The Threshold Line is rising faster than it has before
- The djinn wind-lanes have been unreliable for 18 months
- The Aethon succession crisis has been building for 28 years and shows no sign of resolving
- The Ashkin Assembly has a significant political contest underway involving the Ember faction
- Ascendant creatures have been sighted above the Threshold Line in the Deepward Fringe this year

**What your character does not know:**
- What is causing any of these things
- Whether they are connected
- What Year 100 being the centennial of the Calamity actually means

**Where you start:** The specific circumstances that bring you to Ironedge in Thirdmonth 14 of Year 100 are yours to define with your DM. The Starting Hooks file has threads for each kingdom. Your DM has a specific situation waiting for you.

## Step 6 — Questions for Your Character

These questions will help you build a character who fits naturally into Lunaria's world.

**Foundational:**
1. What do you want that you don't have?
2. What would you refuse to do even for something you desperately want?
3. Who do you trust, and why specifically?
4. What do you know about the Below that you wish you didn't?

**Connection to the world:**
5. Where were you when you first understood that the Threshold Line was rising and what did you do about it?
6. What is your relationship to the Calamity — not theologically, but personally?
7. Have you ever encountered Fragment Fever? In yourself, or someone you cared about?
8. What do you think is actually happening in the world right now?

**For your group:**
9. Why does your character choose to stay involved when things become dangerous?
10. What does your character need from the people around them that they don't ask for directly?
