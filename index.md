# Welcome to Lunaria

> *A world of floating islands, skyships, and secrets beneath the clouds.*

Lunaria is a world of floating islands. There is no ground — or rather, there was ground once, and a century ago something catastrophic happened to it. What remains is **the Below**: a dark, corrupted expanse beneath the islands, rising slowly year by year. The sky-islands are what survived, carried upward by the mineral called **lift-stone** embedded in their rock.

You live, work, trade, and travel between islands. Skyships are the primary transport. The world is a hundred years old in its current form and is still figuring out what it is.

## What Everyone Knows

**The Calamity happened a hundred years ago.** The ground-world ended. The sky-islands rose. Nobody alive was there. Everybody knows someone whose grandparents were.

**The Below is dangerous.** Below the lowest islands, past the Threshold Line, is a corrupted zone that produces hostile creatures, corrupting disease, and fragment-touched phenomena. The Threshold Line is rising. Slowly. Measurably.

**Lift-stone keeps the islands up.** The Stonehollow Compact mines it. The Dominion of Aethon refines it. Every island in the sky depends on this supply chain.

**The djinn wind-lanes are becoming unreliable.** Trade routes that have run for eighty years are showing inconsistencies. Experienced skyship captains have started carrying emergency sails they haven't needed in decades.

**The Ascendant Church says the Calamity was a divine elevation.** The worthy were raised into the sky. The unworthy were not.

## What Nobody Knows (But You Might Find Out)

The world has secrets at its foundations. Some of them are a hundred years old. Some of them are moving.

*Your DM knows what those secrets are.*
