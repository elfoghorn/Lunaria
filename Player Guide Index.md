## How This Vault Works

**Green callout blocks** `[!player]` mark content explicitly confirmed as player-safe.

**Red callout blocks** `[!dm]` mark DM-only knowledge - hidden agendas, true identities, secret histories. Players should not read these sections.

**Everything in this folder** (13_Player_Guide/) is safe to share entirely.

**NPC files** have public information (appearance, public personality, what they do) that is player-safe, and DM sections (secrets, private motivations, what happens if never met) that are not.

**Settlement files** have public information (what the place looks like, who lives there, how it functions) that is player-safe, and Politics/Crime & Shadows sections that are DM-only.

**World system files** (food, travel, banking, religion, disease) are generally player-safe - they describe how the world works. The files on Hidden Power Structures, the full Vasareth Fragments picture, and the Calamity's true cause are DM-only.
