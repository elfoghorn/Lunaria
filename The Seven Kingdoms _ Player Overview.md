# The Seven Kingdoms

Seven sovereign governments control the major island clusters of Lunaria. Each has a distinct political character, cultural identity, and relationship to the sky-world's central crises.

---

## The Dominion of Aethon
*Imperial monarchy. Capital: Aethon Prime (pop. 1.2 million). Ruler: Emperor Valdris III.*

The largest, most powerful, and most politically paralysed kingdom in the sky. The Dominion was the first organised government after the Calamity and has spent a hundred years accumulating institutional authority that it is currently unable to use because the Emperor has not named a successor in twenty-eight years and the High Convocation cannot achieve quorum to act without one.

Aethon holds two world monopolies: lift-stone refining and the largest military fleet. The Ascendant Church is its state faith and its most powerful political institution. Aethon Prime is the world's largest city by significant margin. The Dominion is impressive and currently at an impasse with itself.

**What it's like to be Aethoni:** Hierarchical. The Church is present in daily life whether you want it or not. Institutional loyalty is culturally expected. The succession crisis is discussed in taverns with lowered voices. The south of the kingdom has been quiet in ways that people who pay attention find slightly worrying.

---

## The Conclave of Verath
*Senate republic. Capital: Verath-Ul (pop. 210,000). Ruler: Archon Sylara Thennid (elected).*

The world's knowledge centre. Founded by the scholarly survivors of three pre-Calamity elven city-states, the Conclave built its identity around the Grand Archive - the largest library in the sky - before it built any political structure. Its intelligence network, the Pale Quill, operates the only near-instant long-distance communication in Lunaria through the oracle relay.

The Senate is currently paralysed by the Verath-Senn Declaration, a procedural manoeuvre blocking quorum. Academic debate is vigorous. Political action is not.

**What it's like to be Verathi:** Debate-oriented, legally precise, and deeply uncomfortable with questions that don't have answers. The highest literacy rate of any population in the sky. The most coffee houses per capita. The most formally contested political positions.

---

## The Kairan Sultanate
*Hereditary sultanate. Capital: Nassem's Seat (pop. 460,000). Ruler: Sultan Zariq al-Nassem.*

The world's commercial heart. The Kairan Sultanate controls the djinn wind-lane network that carries sixty percent of inter-kingdom trade. The Grand Bazaar on its own sub-island is the single largest commercial space in the sky. Nassem's Seat is the most cosmopolitan city in Lunaria - fifty thousand non-Kairan permanent residents speaking more than forty languages.

The Sultanate's djinn-contract infrastructure is the basis of its wealth and power, and experienced traders have been noticing for eighteen months that the wind-lanes aren't quite as reliable as they used to be.

**What it's like in the Sultanate:** Commercially energetic, architecturally extraordinary, and genuinely hospitable to outsiders in a way that is both cultural value and commercial strategy. The Grand Bazaar at peak market day is one of the great experiences of the sky-world.

---

## The Stonehollow Compact
*Clan council republic. Capital: Deephold (pop. 95,000 - entirely underground). Ruler: High Speaker Brekka Stonmantle.*

The world's most closed and most internally self-sufficient civilisation. Stonehollow holds the world monopoly on raw lift-stone ore, which gives it structural leverage over every other kingdom that it has never needed to use because it has never needed to use it. The Compact is almost entirely underground - Deephold has no visible buildings on the surface, only ventilation shafts and freight elevator tops.

The Compact's external face is Tradegate, its only above-ground settlement, where outsiders can trade but do not stay long without invitation.

**What it's like in Stonehollow:** Everything is deliberate. The craftsmanship is the best in the world. The hospitality, once extended, is genuine and warm. The culture of ancestor-memory means people carry their dead with them as a daily practice, not a periodic ceremony. Outsiders find the underground disorienting; residents find the surface uncomfortable.

---

## The Sovereign Courts of Myren
*Court monarchy. Capital: Myren's Crown (pop. 195,000). Ruler: Sovereign Veyne.*

The most beautiful kingdom in the sky, governed through seven noble houses who select the Sovereign from among themselves. Myren produces the world's finest silk, the most elaborate court culture, and a genuinely extraordinary cuisine that the other kingdoms grudgingly acknowledge is better than theirs. The tiered island of Myren's Crown - four volcanic plateaus ascending to the Noble Tier's impossible sky-gardens - is one of the most painted subjects in Lunarian art.

Myren's politics are conducted with elaborate courtesy and significant ruthlessness. The social stratification between the four tiers is real and material.

**What it's like in Myren:** The beauty is genuine. The stratification is genuine. The food is extraordinary at every tier. The Noble Tier's social events operate on rules that take months to learn and are enforced by people who have known the rules their whole lives.

---

## The Ashkin Republic
*Democratic republic. Capital: Freehold (pop. 145,000). Ruler: First Speaker Kashar Vel (elected).*

The sky's only democracy, founded forty years ago when tieflings and Warforged who had been enslaved by the Aethon colonial administration organised a three-day revolt, took the garrison, and signed a founding declaration in the administrative hall. The Republic is the youngest kingdom, the smallest major power, and the most politically engaged civilian population in the sky - Assembly sessions are public, gallery queues form the night before significant votes, and political argument is a form of civic participation.

The Dominion of Aethon does not officially recognise the Night of Chains as a slave revolt. The Republic does not officially care what the Dominion calls it.

**What it's like in the Republic:** Egalitarian in ways that other kingdoms find either inspiring or unsettling depending on their politics. Murals on every available surface. The best street food in Lunaria by popular vote. The Wall of Names in Liberation Square is one of the most significant monuments in the sky - a complete record of the Night of Chains, including the people who didn't survive and the people who compromised.

---

## The Verdant Reach
*Elder Circle (dragon-bonded). Primary gathering place: The Great Roost. Lead Elder: Solara.*

Not a kingdom in the conventional sense - a people. The Verdant Reach has no fixed territory, no permanent capital in the architectural sense, no written laws. What it has is an Elder Circle bonded to pre-Calamity Elder dragons, an oral knowledge tradition that makes it the most accurately informed community about the sky's actual condition, and a philosophical commitment to the present moment that makes acting on that knowledge a matter of ongoing community deliberation.

The Reach is the only community in Lunaria with living pre-Calamity witnesses - the Elder dragons. What those dragons know about what happened a hundred years ago and what is happening now is a question several other kingdoms would very much like answered.

**What it's like among the Reach:** The most present-moment culture in the sky. Skill and story are the primary forms of social currency. The Great Roost on a full gathering is overwhelming and extraordinary. Dragon-bonded individuals carry an attunement to their partner that is hard to describe to someone who hasn't witnessed it.
