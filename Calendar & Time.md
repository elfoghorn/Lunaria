# Calendar & Time in Lunaria

## The Post-Calamity Calendar

All dates in Lunaria are counted from Year 0 — the year of the Calamity. The current year is Year 100, which most people regard as a significant threshold. Whether that significance is symbolic or literal is a matter of ongoing debate.

## The Year

The Lunarian year has four seasons of roughly equal length, driven by the sky-current cycles rather than solar orbit — the islands move slowly in relation to the sun, and the seasons reflect the dominant sky-current patterns more than temperature.

**Clear Season** brings the best visibility, peak trade, and lowest storm risk. It is the year's primary commercial and diplomatic season.

**Cloud Season** brings high condensation, peak agricultural yield, and reduced long-distance navigation. It is the farming communities' busiest period.

**Storm Season** carries elevated storm risk across all regions. The Shatter is effectively impassable. Trade slows. Indoor work increases.

**Still Season** brings low wind, more frequent bone-ash drift, and the clearest skies of the year — but also the most Below-adjacent atmospheric intrusion. Fragment Fever cases historically rise during this period.

## The Week and Day

Seven days to the week. The days have names that vary by kingdom — the Dominion uses Church saint-day names; Verath uses scholarly numbering; the Republic uses the founders' names. Most travellers use the Common trade-week: Firstday through Seventhday.

The day is divided into twelve bells, running from dawn. The third bell is midmorning. The sixth bell is midday. The ninth bell is late afternoon. The twelfth bell is midnight.

## Significant Dates

**Year 0** — The Calamity. All calendars begin here.

**Year 1** — Foundation of the Dominion of Aethon; the Compact of the first four island nations.

**Year 3** — Foundation of the Conclave of Verath; the Library Charter signed.

**Year 33** — Compact of Seven signed; Veldren's Cross and The Windbreak declared neutral.

**Year 60** — The Night of Chains; founding of the Ashkin Republic.

**Year 100** — Current year. The centennial of the Calamity.
