# Lunaria Geography - Player Overview

Lunaria's geography is defined by altitude, sky-ocean crossings, and the fact that the entire inhabitable world is suspended in the air above the Below.

---

## The Five Regions

**The Vethara Cluster** - Centre of the known sky. The densest island group, highest average altitude, and most politically significant. Home to Aethon, Verath, and Myren. Large, stable island-continents with rolling sky-plains, the most reliable lift-stone geology, and four centuries of accumulated civilisation. Temperate climate, distinct seasons, the world's most developed commercial and academic infrastructure.

**The Ashenmere** - Left of the Vethara Cluster, slightly lower altitude. Rocky, scarred, more numerous smaller islands. The sky here carries bone-ash from the Below on still days - residents have learned to filter their water after heavy ash-drift. Home to Stonehollow and the Ashkin Republic. The most direct experience of the Calamity's aftermath is here; the Below is closer, the geology harder, the people correspondingly tougher.

**The Sundrift Reaches** - A warm arc of islands upper-right of the Vethara Cluster. The Kairan Sultanate occupies the inner Reaches; the outer Reaches are too vast and diverse for any single power to govern. The Sundrift warm current carries fine amber mineral dust through the region, giving sunsets here a distinctive gold. The world's most commercially active region, warmest climate, most diverse sky-fauna.

**The Coldspire Altitudes** - Above all other regions, above most weather systems, too cold and too high for permanent settlement. Crystal-stone formations and ice-capped islands in permanent frigid silence. Three Aethon colonisation attempts failed. What lives there, if anything, is not catalogued. The Coldspire Cathedral - a crystal-stone natural formation whose interior light forms seventeen standing figures at dawn and dusk - is the most significant unexplored site in Lunaria.

**The Deepward Fringe** - Not a cluster but a band: the lowest inhabited altitudes, wrapping the bottom of the habitable sky. Small, irregular islands closest to the Threshold Line. Fringe communities are the most resilient and the least studied people in Lunaria. They know things about the Below that academic institutions are still trying to formalise.

## The Four Sky-Oceans

**The Calm** - The primary trade corridor between the Vethara Cluster and the Sundrift Reaches. Sixty percent of inter-kingdom commercial traffic. Kairan djinn-lanes make it the most reliably navigable sky-ocean. The Windbreak waystation sits at the midpoint - neutral Compact of Seven territory, every political persuasion present, the best intelligence exchange in the sky if you know how to listen.

**The Shatter** - A permanent storm corridor between the Ashenmere and the Sundrift Reaches. The shortest route between those regions saves twelve days over the long way around. Experienced pilots thread through in specific weather windows. Inexperienced ones don't make the attempt more than once. Storm-Kraaken have been documented here.

**The Blind** - The western uncharted expanse. Navigation instruments behave unreliably. Forty-three islands confirmed; sixteen populated, some with languages traceable to pre-Calamity civilisations. The Verdant Reach navigates it by dragon-instinct. Everyone else navigates it by courage and hope. The Blind-Whale - an ancient, non-corrupted creature of extraordinary size - moves through the Blind's lower altitudes.

**The Undertow Deeps** - The sky between the lowest inhabited islands and the Threshold Line. Not a trade route - nothing that goes there on purpose stays there long. Ascendant creatures have been confirmed above the Threshold Line in the Deeps since Year 97.

## Travel Times (approximate)

| Route | Via | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Aethon Prime → Nassem's Seat | The Calm, djinn-lane | 4-6 days |
| Aethon Prime → Verath-Ul | Vethara Cluster, direct | 2 days |
| Nassem's Seat → Freehold | Calm then Ashenmere | 8-10 days |
| Nassem's Seat → Great Roost | Sundrift inner lanes | 5 days |
| Freehold → Deephold | Ashenmere direct | 3 days |
| Any point → Coldspire | Long and dangerous | 2-3 weeks |

*Times vary significantly with weather, djinn-lane availability, and ship quality.*

## The Below - What Is Publicly Known

The Below is the corrupted remnant of the pre-Calamity ground-world. It is dark, hostile, and rising. The Threshold Line - its upper boundary - moves upward at approximately two metres per month by current measurements.

The Below produces Ascendant creatures that are increasingly appearing above the Threshold Line in the Undertow Deeps. It produces Below-Corruption disease that requires specialist treatment. Fringe communities and Seekers' Concord Runners operate in and near it as a professional practice. Most people in the major kingdoms have never seen it.
