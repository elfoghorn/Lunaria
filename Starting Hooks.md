# Starting Hooks

Ways into the campaign. These are the threads a player character might plausibly be pulling on at the start of play.

---

## Hooks by Kingdom

### Aethon
- **The Succession**: You have a financial, political, or personal stake in who becomes the next Emperor. You've been trying to get a message to one of the three claimants without being noticed.
- **The Sealed District**: Someone you care about went into the Sealed District before it was sealed in Year 61. The record says they didn't come out. The record is forty years old and was written by people who had reasons to manage the information carefully.
- **The Southern Islands**: You live on or have family on one of the southern Ashfield islands. Something is wrong with the crops. The Church assessor gave a satisfactory answer. The crops don't know the answer was satisfactory.

### Verath
- **The Archive**: You've been trying to access a specific document in the Grand Archive for months. The request keeps being deferred without explanation. The document is about something that happened before the Calamity.
- **The Oracle**: You paid for an oracle consultation. What you received was not what you asked about. It was more specific and more urgent and it used your name in a way the oracle shouldn't have known.
- **The Senate**: You work for or are adjacent to the reform movement. Someone has been interfering with the reform bloc's communications. Not intercepting - modifying.

### Kairan
- **The Wind-Lanes**: Your commercial operation depends on the djinn wind-lanes running on schedule. They haven't been running on schedule. You've been trying to find out why without triggering a market panic.
- **The Bazaar**: You operate in the Grand Bazaar's eastern expansion. You've been asked to transport something from a buyer you can't identify to a destination you can't verify. The price is very good.
- **The Contract Vaults**: You are a contract practitioner. A djinn you've worked with for years said something to you that you're still processing. It wasn't hostile. It was specific. It contained a location.

### Stonehollow
- **The Survey**: You were supposed to receive word from someone two weeks ago. The word hasn't come. You've been told this is a standard delay. You know this person and standard delays are not their pattern.
- **The Deep**: You work in the Deepforge. Level 15 has been warmer for three months. You filed the report. The acknowledgment didn't contain a response. You've been thinking about going to look.
- **Trade Gate**: You move goods through Tradegate. Someone has been purchasing copies of export manifests through secondary channels - not the manifests themselves, copies. You're trying to understand why before deciding whether to mention it.

### Myren
- **The Tiers**: You're from Lowergate. You have data - six years of Threshold Line measurements - that the Noble Tier has been told about twice and called "under review" twice. You're trying to find someone with standing to make it matter.
- **The Estate**: You work at, trade with, or have connections to one of the noble estates. Something changed at House Torvaine in the past year. The change is visible if you know what to look for. Most people aren't looking.
- **The Missing**: Someone from Myren's Crown disappeared fourteen months ago. The family has been asking questions. The questions have been arriving at the wrong doors. You know where the right door is and have been deciding whether to knock.

### Ashkin Republic
- **The Assembly**: You are a delegate, a researcher, or a citizen who attends sessions. A vote is coming that will determine the Ember faction's proposal. You have information that should change that vote. Getting it into the room without being dismissed as a political operative is the problem.
- **The Ember Quarter**: You believe in the Republic's founding principles and you're watching the Ember faction's energy with increasing concern. Something about the planning room meetings has changed. The people coming out of them are more certain than they should be about things that don't yet have answers.
- **The Wall**: You have a name on the Wall of Names. You have been carrying something about that name - a question, a debt, a piece of information - and you've been deciding for a long time what to do with it.

### Verdant Reach
- **The Ledge**: You are a Reach community member. Vyrreth's ledge has been empty for eighteen months and it is still warm. The Elder Circle has not explained this. You have been waiting for the explanation and it has not come, and the community's patience with waiting is approaching a limit.
- **The Half-Bonded**: You are near-bonded, or you know someone who is. The directional pull has been getting stronger. You've been sketching where it points. The sketches form a pattern that points somewhere specific and deep.
- **The Market**: You trade at Skyveil Market. The Trade Stone's carvings have been changing. You've been documenting this. You showed your documentation to someone who looked at it for a long time and said "keep documenting" and nothing else, and you've been thinking about that response ever since.

## Cross-Kingdom Hooks

- **The Fragment**: You found, received, or were given a fragment shard (Shard 2 or lower). It's warm. It's been getting warmer. You're trying to find out what it is before deciding what to do with it.
- **The Message**: You're carrying a sealed message for someone you haven't been able to find. You've had it for weeks. You have the address. The address doesn't correspond to any building you can locate in any city you've visited.
- **The Dream**: You've been having the same dream for two months. The dream is a specific location - a place you've never been, that you can navigate in the dream with certainty. You've been trying to identify the location from the geographic details. You're getting close.
