# Welcome to Lunaria

Lunaria is a world of floating islands. There is no ground - or rather, there was ground once, and a century ago something catastrophic happened to it. What remains of the ground is the Below: a dark, corrupted expanse beneath the islands, rising slowly year by year. The sky-islands are what survived, carried upward by the mineral called lift-stone embedded in their rock.

You live, work, trade, and travel between islands. Skyships are the primary transport. The wind-lanes that make long-distance travel reliable are maintained by the Kairan Sultanate's djinn-contract infrastructure. The world is a hundred years old in its current form and is still figuring out what it is.

Seven kingdoms govern the major island clusters. Dozens of independent settlements, waystation communities, and Fringe communities exist between and below them. The world is connected enough to have international commerce, diplomatic agreements, and shared crises - and fragmented enough that a week's travel separates one cultural reality from another.

## What Everyone Knows

**The Calamity happened a hundred years ago.** The ground-world ended. The sky-islands rose. Nobody alive was there. Everybody knows someone whose grandparents were.

**The Below is dangerous.** Below the lowest islands, past the Threshold Line, is a corrupted zone that produces hostile creatures, corrupting disease, and fragment-touched phenomena. The Threshold Line - the boundary between the sky and the Below - is rising. Slowly. Measurably. This is not a secret, but nobody in a position of power is publicly discussing what it means.

**Lift-stone keeps the islands up.** The Stonehollow Compact mines it. The Dominion of Aethon refines it. Every island in the sky depends on this supply chain, which makes the political relationship between those two kingdoms the most structurally important in the world.

**The djinn wind-lanes are becoming unreliable.** Trade routes that have run for eighty years are showing inconsistencies. The Kairan Sultanate says it is being addressed. Experienced skyship captains have started carrying emergency sails they haven't needed in decades.

**The Ascendant Church says the Calamity was a divine elevation.** The worthy were raised into the sky. The unworthy were not. This theology is the Dominion of Aethon's state faith, which means it is politically very powerful and geographically specific. Outside the Dominion, views on the Calamity's nature vary considerably.

## What Nobody Knows (But You Might Find Out)

The world has secrets at its foundations. Some of them are a hundred years old. Some of them are moving.

*Your DM knows what those secrets are.*
