# The Below

This is the publicly available knowledge about the Below. What lies at its depths, what it means for the future of Lunaria, and who knows what about its current state — that is a different question.

## What It Is

The Below is what remains of the pre-Calamity ground-world. A hundred years ago, the sky-islands rose. What didn't rise stayed — the rocks, the ruins, the old world's remnants — and became the Below over the century that followed. It is dark, corrupted, and inhabited by things that did not exist before the Calamity.

## The Threshold Line

The Threshold Line is the boundary between the sky and the Below — the upper limit of Below-corruption. It is visible from the Deepward Fringe islands as a membrane-like boundary in the air below the lowest islands: the point where the sky ends and the corruption begins.

The Threshold Line is rising. Current public measurements — maintained by Fringe communities and several academic institutions — show it rising approximately two metres per month. The lowest inhabited islands have margins measured in years, not decades. Most kingdoms are aware of this. Most governing bodies are not publicly discussing the implications.

## Below-Corruption

The primary health hazard of Below proximity. A condition affecting living tissue exposed to Below-adjacent atmosphere, particularly in the Bloom Zone (the upper Below). Below-Corruption progresses through documented stages.

**Stage 0-1:** Mild exposure. Reversible with prompt treatment. The Verath Standard Protocol has an 82% arrest rate at this stage. Symptoms include disorientation, faint silver markings on skin that fade with distance from the Below, and mild directional sense disturbances.

**Stage 2:** Significant exposure. More difficult to arrest. Requires specialist treatment — the Verath-Lor Treatment Centre is the primary facility. Stage 2 shows permanent minor physical changes even when arrested: translucent patches of skin at the exposure site, permanent minor fragment-sense.

**Stage 3:** Severe exposure. Very few documented reversals. Specialist intervention required immediately. The Healer's Compound at Dust-Harbour handles referrals.

**Fragment Fever:** A separate condition caused by proximity to high-concentration Vasareth fragment material. Produces vivid navigation visions, silver pupil markings, and in late stages a directional certainty that experienced practitioners describe as the most disturbing symptom because the direction is consistent and specific. Treatable at Stage 1 with high success rates.

## Below Creatures

**Ascendants** are the primary Below-creature threat at surface levels. Disc-shaped and fast, they operate in patterns that suggest coordination. Scout-class Ascendants have been confirmed above the Threshold Line in the Undertow Deeps since Year 97. Their population appears to be growing. The Seekers' Concord maintains the most current classification and threat assessment.

**The Undead Tide** is the collective term for Below-adapted remnants of pre-Calamity life — the Calcified, the Congregants, the Remembering. Found in the Bloom Zone and below. They do not pursue into the sky above the Threshold Line. This is the one reliable fact about them.

**Corrupted Fauna** are surface animals that have descended past the Threshold Line and returned changed — or that have been born in the Bloom Zone. Recognisable, but wrong. Below-adjacent Ash-Crawlers appearing at higher altitudes than standard are the most commonly encountered example.

## The Runners

The Seekers' Concord Runners are the professionals who operate in and near the Below. They maintain wayhouses in Fringe communities, carry messages between kingdoms via routes that don't use the oracle relay, and conduct Below-entry expeditions for academic and commercial clients.

Runner neutrality is institutional — they take contracts from any party without political preference. Their below-entry protocols, equipment standards, and operational knowledge are the most practically useful body of Below-information available. The Concord's published guides to Below-entry preparation are available at any Concord wayhouse.

## What You Might Find Below

Pre-Calamity ruins are the primary draw for Below expeditions. The old world's cities — the Writhing Cities, as they're called — are preserved in the Below's specific conditions and represent the largest archive of pre-Calamity material culture in existence. Most academic Below expeditions are funded for archaeological recovery.

Fragment material — pieces of the shattered god Vasareth, embedded in Below rock since the Calamity — is the most commercially valuable Below material. It is also among the most dangerous to handle without proper containment. The Dust-Harbour Shard Market is the primary legal-adjacent marketplace.

*The Ascendant Church teaches that the Below is the natural resting place of those who were not elevated during the Calamity — the unworthy, now permanent inhabitants of what they deserved. Entering the Below, by this theology, is a form of spiritual transgression. This view is not universal even within the Dominion. It is not held at all in most of the rest of Lunaria.*
