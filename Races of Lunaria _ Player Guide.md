# Races of Lunaria

Lunaria has all the standard races plus three that are unique to the sky-world's conditions. Here's what players would commonly know about each.

## Humans

The most numerous race in the sky. Pre-Calamity humans were the *newest* major civilisation — younger than elven city-states, younger than dwarven clans. Post-Calamity, sheer population translated into dominance. Three distinct cultures have emerged.

**Aethoni** are imperial humans, shaped by Church doctrine, hierarchy, and the Mandate of Ascension. The Ashfield frontier farmers are meaningfully different from their urban counterparts: harder, more independent, and quietly sympathetic to the Republic in ways the High Convocation prefers not to examine.

**Verathi** are scholar humans, shaped by Verath's debate culture. Legally precise and deeply uncomfortable with unanswerable questions.

**Fringe Humans** are the people who live closest to the Below. Denser build, documented resistance to early-stage corruption exposure, no patience for ideology.

*What other races think:* Elves note that humans build fast and forget faster. Dwarves say they have good ideas and no patience. Tieflings have complicated views on humans that are worth asking about directly.

## Elves

Three sub-races, distinct enough in culture to be almost separate peoples.

**High Elves** are the institutional memory of Lunaria. They disproportionately occupy Verath's senior positions, Myren's noble houses, and the Pale Quill's leadership. They build systems and live long enough to see what those systems do when nobody's paying attention.

**Shadow Elves** possess patience at a scale that is genuinely difficult for shorter-lived races to process. They don't hurry. They don't consider urgency a virtue. Some of the most significant political operators in the sky are Shadow Elves whose strategies span decades.

**Wood Elves** are present-tense focused, which among elves is a distinct cultural statement. "What is needed now" rather than "what is the hundred-year strategy." Found everywhere, in charge of almost nothing by institutional measure and comfortable with this.

*Lifespan note:* Every High Elf above 200 years old was alive for Year 0. They remember the Calamity personally. This is not a small thing.

## Dwarves

**Mountain Dwarves** are the Compact's external-facing culture. Commercially sophisticated, institutionally stubborn, and possessed of a long memory expressed as professional relationship standards. Commercial relationships with Mountain Dwarves operate on a centuries-long trust-building timeline.

**Shield Dwarves** carry the Compact's security and structural engineering culture. Protective function as identity. They hold positions so others can hold positions behind them.

**Deep Dwarves** represent the Compact's deep-level culture, distinct enough to be effectively a separate people. Smaller, adapted to complete darkness, with wide pupils and no beard tradition. They know things about the deep rock of Lunaria that other communities don't and haven't been asked about enough.

## Aarakocra & Tabaxi

The primary peoples of the Verdant Reach, though both are found across all kingdoms.

**Aarakocra** are the sky-born. Flight is not a feature for them — it is the baseline. Their culture is built around the freedom of three-dimensional movement that no other race has natively. Clan identity is strong; the Elder Circle that governs the Verdant Reach is Aarakocra-led.

**Tabaxi** are the trackers and storytellers. Skill and story are the primary forms of social currency. A Tabaxi who has a good story is richer than one who doesn't, regardless of material circumstances. They collect experiences with the same energy that other cultures collect material goods.

## Halflings, Gnomes & Dragonborn

**Halflings** are Lunaria's network people — found in every city, every waystation, every commercial district. No primary kingdom of their own; community organised by extended family networks that span kingdoms. The most comprehensive informal information network in the sky operates through Halfling social connections.

**Gnomes** are the Conclave's inventors. Rock Gnomes dominate precision manufacturing; Forest Gnomes maintain a connection to sky-ecology that produces unusual botanical and naturalist expertise. The world's most valuable single instruments are certified by Gnome artisans.

**Dragonborn** are not a discrete population but a cultural description — those with enough draconic heritage to form or strengthen dragon-bonds. Found primarily in and around the Verdant Reach, where dragon-bond culture provides a community context for their specific capabilities.

## Tieflings & Warforged

**Tieflings** are the founding population of the Ashkin Republic and have communities in every major city. The Night of Chains — the revolt that founded the Republic — is their founding story, and they carry it with them regardless of where they live. The Ashen Tiefling heritage (distinct silver-grey markings, amber luminescent eyes) is the most common in Lunaria; other lineages exist.

**Warforged** are self-made — constructed before the Calamity, adapted since. The only people in Lunaria who were alive for Year 0 and can remember it continuously are Warforged and Elder dragons. The Iron Archive in Ironrest holds 186 years of Warforged contributed memory. Their relationship to the Ashkin Republic is constitutionally interesting and practically cooperative.

## World-Specific Races

**Ashwalkers** are not a separate species but an adaptive lineage — humans (and occasionally others) whose families have lived near the Below threshold for multiple generations. Silver-grey skin tint, luminescent eyes in low light, documented fragment-sense (the ability to detect fragment concentration as warmth, pressure, or directional pull). The Seekers' Concord Runners actively recruit Ashwalkers. The Ascendant Church has not formally classified them.

**Veilborn** are born from Threshold events — rare, roughly one per decade in the entire sky. Silver luminescent eyes, always on. They perceive the dead — not visually exactly, but as a sensory awareness of recent death and emotional residue. Twenty-three confirmed living Veilborn. Every major intelligence service wants access to one. The experience of being Veilborn is described by most as a continuous low-level grief that becomes manageable rather than absent with time.

**Stonekith** are ancient stone beings in Stonehollow's depths. Most people in Lunaria have never heard of them. Most people who have heard of them assume they're a myth.
